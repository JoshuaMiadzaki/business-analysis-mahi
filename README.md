# Business Analysis & Process Management (Coursera)

📌 This repository contains my coursework and final project for the **"Business Analysis & Process Management"** course on Coursera by Mahi.

---

## 🎯 Course Highlights

- How to define a business identity and identify business needs
- Creating and analyzing business process models
- Working with Lucidchart to design flowcharts and swim lanes
- Evaluating and improving business workflows

---

## 🛠 Project: Mahi's Styling Business

I modeled a real-world business process for a fictional interior styling startup.  
This included decision logic and client journey breakdown across service packages (Silver, Bronze, Gold).

### 🧩 Included files:
- [`mahi_styling_process.pdf`](./mahi_styling_process.pdf): Final diagram
- [`mahi_styling_process.json`](./mahi_styling_process.json): Raw model data

---

## 🧠 What I learned

- Visual thinking for business processes
- How to structure roles with swim lanes
- Identifying decision points and optimizations
- Communicating process logic clearly and effectively
